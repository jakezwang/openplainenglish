# Code of Conduct

OpenPlainEnglish welcomes people with different language backgrounds, reading abilities, disabilities, health experiences, and technical skills. Everyone should be able to ask a basic question, explain confusion, and contribute without being belittled.

## How we participate

- Use respectful, clear language. Discuss an idea or behavior without attacking a person.
- Listen when someone says wording is confusing or a tool is inaccessible.
- Welcome corrections and explain disagreement with evidence.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not post private health information or identifying details without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and make room for people to participate.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, repeated personal insults, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are unacceptable. Do not use project discussions to give unsolicited personal diagnoses or pressure someone about their health choices.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit a discussion, warn a participant, or restrict participation. They will consider the behavior, its impact, and the need to protect contributors, and should explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

To flag public conduct, use GitHub's reporting controls or contact a repository maintainer through a contact route they have made public. Do not publish private incident details in an issue. This repository does not currently publish a separate confidential conduct-reporting address. GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for abuse on GitHub.
