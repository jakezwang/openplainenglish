Help with selected phrases.

Plain reading uses reviewed phrase pairs. It does not rewrite every sentence or understand every context. “Explain terms” keeps original wording with explanations. You decide when to start and stop.

Keyboard: Tab reaches marked phrases, Enter opens an explanation, and Escape closes it. A new page always starts with reading off.
